S4-QA1 End-to-end demo script (staging, one pass, screenshots at each step)
Step 1: rep places a scheduled-delivery order for the test shop. Order page shows PendingApproval.
Step 2: agency operator approves. Order Confirmed; Deliveries shows a new Pending delivery for it; the order page's delivery panel links to it.
Step 3: operator assigns the territory's rep for tomorrow. Status Assigned, rep and date shown.
Step 4: rep opens My deliveries, starts the delivery. Status In Transit; shop and agency get the In transit email.
Step 5: rep marks it delivered. Shop and agency get the Delivered email.
Step 6: shop owner sees "Awaiting your confirmation" and confirms receipt.
Step 7: shop owner raises a shop return of 5 units with a reason. Agency's Shop returns list shows it pending.
Step 8: operator approves. Inventory page shows the agency's stock up by 5.
Step 9: Company Admin opens Audit trail from the order page. OrderPlaced, OrderApproved, OrderConfirmed, DeliveryJobCreated, DeliveryStatusChanged x3, DeliveryConfirmed, ReturnRequested, ReturnAccepted appear in time order. Verify integrity is green.
Step 10: Reports for this month include the order in sales, order count and the agency breakdown.
Step 11 (Sprint 3 path): rep completes a cash sale with GPS check-in and payment; the audit trail shows OrderPlaced, OrderConfirmed, PaymentRecorded with the check-in coordinates and the map link.
